## The update

Mr Dimitriou introduces 3D ophthalmic education and acknowledges Stewart Mardell’s team and support from Haag-Streit. The announcement initially proposed 3 and 4 October 2024 for iGLEAMS 3.

Those provisional dates were superseded by the published programme, which listed 30 September and 1 October 2024. This is an archived announcement, not a current invitation. [Read the later programme summary](/articles/igleams-3-surgical-education/).
