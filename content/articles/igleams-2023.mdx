## The second symposium

The original website records iGLEAMS 2 on 12 and 13 May 2023 at the ICENI Centre and the University of Essex Business School. Mr Dimitriou chaired and organised the meeting.

## Practical learning

The first day included simulation across 12 stations. The second brought together expert presentations, 3D surgical video and interactive discussion. Faculty included Sheng Lim, Tarek Shaarawy, André Mermoud, Imran Masood, Dan Lindfield, Andrew Tatham, Gok Ratnarajan and Ian Rodrigues, with a visualisation contribution from Lukan Mishev.
