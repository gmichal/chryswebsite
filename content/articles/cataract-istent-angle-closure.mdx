## A focused clinical question

This multicentre cohort study compared cataract surgery alone with cataract surgery combined with an iStent in primary angle-closure glaucoma. Chrysostomos Dimitriou is listed among the co-authors.

## The evidence type

The study retrospectively examined 185 eyes. It considered pressure control, medication use and complications. An observational comparison cannot by itself establish which approach is right for an individual patient.

## Read the original research

The complete paper sets out the eligibility criteria, analysis and limitations. This brief is a bibliographic introduction, not a recommendation for routine use of a particular implant in angle-closure glaucoma.
