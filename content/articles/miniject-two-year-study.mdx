## The research question

This paper examined MINIject implantation in people with medically uncontrolled primary open-angle glaucoma. It assessed eye pressure, medication use and adverse events over 24 months.

## Study design

The authors reviewed records for 37 eyes: 27 had combined cataract surgery and MINIject implantation, while 10 received the implant alone. Chrysostomos Dimitriou is a co-author alongside Panagiotis Dervenis, Nikolaos Dervenis and Gerassimos Lascaratos.

## Reading the evidence

This is a retrospective study, rather than a randomised comparison. Its findings describe the study population and do not establish an individual treatment outcome. The authors identify the need for longer follow-up. The original paper provides the results, methods and limitations in full.

iSTAR Medical, the manufacturer of MINIject, lists Mr Dimitriou as a scientific adviser. The manufacturer’s profile is included in the references.
