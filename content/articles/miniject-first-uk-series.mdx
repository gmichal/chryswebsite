## About the publication

Chrysostomos Dimitriou and colleagues reported a retrospective, single-centre series of 50 eyes treated with the MINIject glaucoma implant. The paper includes standalone procedures and procedures combined with cataract surgery, with outcomes assessed at six months.

## What the study examined

The research considered eye pressure, medication use and adverse events. This brief identifies the study and its scope; it does not reproduce treatment-success percentages or recommend a device.

## Context and limitations

The limited follow-up and observational design matter when interpreting the findings. Read the complete publication for its methods, results and disclosures. Different numerical summaries within the paper require reconciliation before they are used in patient-facing explanations.

## Professional disclosure

iSTAR Medical, the manufacturer of MINIject, lists Mr Dimitriou as a scientific adviser. The manufacturer’s profile is included in the references.
