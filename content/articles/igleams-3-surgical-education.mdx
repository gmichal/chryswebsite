## The 2024 programme

Chrys Dimitriou and Dan Lindfield introduced the third iGLEAMS symposium in a professional education article. The programme combined presentations and 3D surgical video at Firstsite with practical simulation at the ICENI Centre in Colchester.

## Learning through discussion and practice

The introduction described a meeting for surgeons at different stages of experience, with discussion of glaucoma research, imaging and microsurgical techniques. The published dates were 30 September and 1 October 2024.

## An archive, not a current invitation

This is a historical programme summary. The source’s registration invitation and accreditation details relate to that edition. Current courses should be checked directly with the ICENI Centre.
