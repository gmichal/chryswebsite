## iGLEAMS historical material

The original page says EAGLAMS was founded on 10 October 2020 and later renamed iGLEAMS. The first symposium took place on 20-21 May 2022. The second took place on 12-13 May 2023 at the ICENI Centre and Essex Business School, University of Essex. The page describes over 200 delegates across the first two editions, 12 simulation stations, three rooms, national and international speakers, 3D demonstrations and presentation awards. It announces a third event for 3-4 October 2024 and a trainee session called MiGS FEAST. These are historical dates in 2026.

The downloaded 12-page PDF is the **2023** programme, not a current event brochure. The cover was rendered and visually inspected, and text was extracted from all pages. It contains conference information, device sponsor advertisements and a fellowship vacancy that closed on 22 May 2023. Device advertising is not independent clinical evidence and must not be transformed into the surgeon's advice. Page 9 verifies Chrys Dimitriou's 2023 session about MINIject and the suprachoroidal space. Page 11 describes the historical ICENI international fellowship in glaucoma and complex cataract surgery. The brochure's foundation date wording is 2022, which should be interpreted as the first symposium rather than overriding the website's 2020 foundation account without review.
