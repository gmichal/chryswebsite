Completed 23 October 2026 at the owner's request to include every discovered record.

All **63 inventoried LinkedIn records** now map one-to-one to published MDX entries: **30 posts or authored-post excerpts, 25 third-party mentions and 8 comments**. Together with six other existing entries, the archive contains **69 published entries**. The original professional-recognition summary represents LI-002; LI-003 was rewritten as a bounded commentary summary and published. The other 61 entries are new files.

This is full coverage of the discovery inventory, not a complete LinkedIn account export or 63 full-text reproductions. None of the exact LinkedIn publication dates was verified. Search-cache ages were not converted into dates. Missing post links use their recorded discovery profile/company URL and are labelled as such. Website text is credited to the archive, with original source authors credited separately.

The public archive includes a native source-type filter, combined with subject and search. Third-party awards and appointments retain their recipients; historical invitations are not active registration offers. The two potentially overlapping UNITY CS records are both included with the ambiguity disclosed. LI-048 is a source note without patient identity, quotations, images or medical details. LI-062 explicitly leaves authorship unresolved. No missing attachments, transcripts, outcome figures or medical conclusions were invented.

Public post text was rechecked where accessible for equipment, congress, education and colleague-update records. Some earlier indexed discoveries remain excerpt-only. Sources are linked in each MDX file and in the inventory. Previous hold/exclusion decisions remain in each inventory record's `previousDisposition` as an audit trail; the current `finalDisposition`, `publicationStatus` and `resultingSlug` record the inclusion.
